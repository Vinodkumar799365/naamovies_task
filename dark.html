DARK

The term "dark" can refer to many things, but most search results relate to the critically
acclaimed German science fiction thriller TV series Dark.

The absence of light: The common definition of the word, opposite to light.
Dark matter: A hypothetical form of matter that accounts for a large part of the universe and does not
emit light or energy, which scientists are actively researching.
A film or book title: There are other movies and creative works titled "Dark" or that explore dark themes.
